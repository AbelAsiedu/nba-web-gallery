This gallery is about the National Basketball Association (NBA), the top professional basketball league in North America. It brings together eleven pictures: nine of players in the jerseys of well-known teams such as the Los Angeles Lakers, the Boston Celtics, the Chicago Bulls, the Golden State Warriors, the Los Angeles Clippers and the Philadelphia 76ers, and two of the arenas where NBA basketball is played: Madison Square Garden in New York City and the Chase Center in San Francisco.

The NBA was chosen as the theme because basketball is one of the most popular sports in the world, and the league is followed by millions of fans around the world. Visitors will see the pictures arranged in four sections: the Lakers legends, champions from Boston, Chicago and Golden State, stars in action, and famous arenas. Under every picture you will find the player's name, position, team and what he is famous for, and a table near the end gathers the same facts in one place. The subject matters because the NBA shows how teamwork, skill and hard work can inspire people of every age.
