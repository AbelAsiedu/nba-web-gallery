This page is a small collection of six short video clips from National Basketball Association (NBA) games. The clips are saved inside the Videos folder of this project and are played with the HTML video tag, so they work without an internet connection. The theme is NBA highlights, and the clips are arranged in three parts: powerful dunks, shooting, and defence through blocked shots.

The purpose of the collection is to show the different skills that make basketball exciting to watch. Viewers can enjoy the athletic dunks, watch a Golden State Warriors game from a post about Stephen Curry's shooting, and see how a defender can stop a shot at the last moment. Every video has a heading and a short description, and a table near the end gives the key facts about each clip. All the videos play automatically and repeat (autoplay and loop, with the sound muted because browsers only allow autoplay when the sound is off), and the controls are switched on so a viewer can unmute, pause and move through each video.
